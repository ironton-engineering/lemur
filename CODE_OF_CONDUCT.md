# Code of Conduct

Lemur contributors must provide a safe and respectful project space.

Be clear, patient, and constructive. Discuss technical work without personal attacks. Do not use harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, or publication of private information.

Maintainers can edit or remove harmful content. They can limit participation when behavior harms the project or its users.

Report conduct problems through the private method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will protect the privacy of a good-faith report as far as practical.
